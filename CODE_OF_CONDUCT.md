# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Argue with the claim, never with the person making it. Being wrong is part of the research
process and is treated as such here.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume competence and good faith, including from people newer than you
- Criticise the technical content, the evidence and the reasoning
- Cite sources when you disagree, and accept correction when you are shown to be wrong
- Respect that maintainers review in their own time

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone's experience level
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information
- Deliberately derailing discussion, or repeatedly reopening settled decisions without new
  evidence

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially. Depending on severity the
response ranges from a warning to a permanent ban from the project's spaces.

Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith may face the same consequences as
anyone else.
